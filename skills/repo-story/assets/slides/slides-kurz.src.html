The pattern

Every mishap became a check, not a warning

A 4 TB crash→a fuzz test
“Looks right”→a pixel oracle
Red formatting→a CI step
Broken layering→a build failure
Agent file access→an allow-list

Agents may be fast, but cannot get far without something tripping.

What we take away

Five questions for your own product

  1. Can an agent do everything a human does in the UI?
  2. Is our interface a thin client on top of that layer?
  3. Can an agent perceive our system, or does it guess?
  4. How do we secure its access?
  5. Do we have an acceptance test that plays through flows with agents?
