Code Fitness Bubbles

Hierarchical bubbles: a folder is a circle around its files and subfolders. Click a bubble to zoom in, click the background or the breadcrumb to go back. Fitness = smell score against the weights in weights.yaml, our own heuristic. "Unfit and changing" shows red and yellow files by how often they changed in the window; fit files stay grey. Hover shows the smells. Layout after the zoomable circle packing of the Code-Hotspot-Analyse (feststelltaste). As of: __META__
